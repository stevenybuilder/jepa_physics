Part 2

Spline and manifold steering

An edit should follow the shape a variable has in the encoder, and it should be judged by what the world model does with it.

A  What shape?

B  Which edit, at held-out targets?

C  Does the predictor obey it?

D  Does it generalise?

~10 s. Four questions in order. Shape: ring for direction, lines for speed and acceleration, a cone when both vary. Edit: spline against chord, probe directions, conceptor gates and a learned twin-difference edit, all at the same norm and at values the spline never saw. Judge: the predictor's forecast, not a probe at the edited layer. Generalisation: across renders and across objects.
