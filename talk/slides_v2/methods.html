Methods · how we constructed the curves

~40 s. Sources: REPORT §2; setup and design notes. How we built the curves. First, PCA to the 64 main directions on training clips only. Second, one mean per label value. Third, a closed, periodic spline through the means for direction, an open, natural spline for speed and acceleration. Fourth, the edit slides a clip's activation along the curve to the target and keeps its off-curve residual. The straight edit moves between the same means in a line, in the same 64-D subspace. Probes are ridge regressions with an 80/20 split stratified by value and five-fold cross-validation inside train, read at 26 depths. Three disjoint clip sets keep the judge honest: 632 clips build the curve, 480 fit the evaluation probe, 300 are steered. Controls: a dose-matched line, random curves and shuffled means.
