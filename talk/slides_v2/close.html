Closing question

Can we steer a world model's internal reasoning trace, one physical variable at a time?

Joseph's shift: ask not only what the model predicted but how it arrived there, through its chain of latent variables.

Steering along the representation's own geometry is fine-grained steering of that chain: one physical variable, at the depth the predictor reads it.

Reading the forecast's variables back is white-box evaluation. Together they are a chain of thought for physical AI: edit the state, read the outcome, before a robot acts.

Sources: Joseph, "World models and interpretability are two sides of the same coin" (soniajoseph.ai): "The world model is the internal state variables and dynamics learned by the foundation model itself"; "We want access to the model's internal reasoning trace– the chain of latent variables that combined to produce the next action, prediction, or frame"; "The second reformulation is white-box evaluation.
