Comparison with the multi-probe method

Against Part 1's multi-probe subspace: the probe edit lands as close, but pushes harder and leaves the ring on the way

Held-out headings, 16 seeds over 15 arcs, blocks 12 and 22.

Where the edit lands

A tie.

Both reach a heading the fit never saw, within a few degrees of each other.

How it gets there

The curve wins.

The probe edit is a straight line through the ring's hollow: halfway there it carries no heading. The curve keeps one the whole way, and the forecast follows the curve.

What it costs

The probe edit pushes 1.5 times harder.

At the curve's size it misses by 23°. It needs no coordinate, which is its strength; the curve needs one it can trust.

What both need

A late block, or the disk's tokens.

Placed mid-depth and frame-wide, either edit is repaired before the predictor reads it.
