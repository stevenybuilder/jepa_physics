Sources: refs/physics_paper.txt l.795–798 and Table 1 (l.85–95); refs/steering_paper.txt §7. ~50 s. Joseph et al. established layer-wise readability and their central construct, the Physics Emergence Zone at blocks 8 to 9, and that direction "spans tens of approximately orthogonal components". In C.1.4 they left open whether "velocity information is most ”bound” to the corresponding object". Because their steering is judged by a probe at the edited layer, they never test whether the predictor uses the code. Wurgaft et al. established, on language and RL tasks, that steering along a fitted manifold gives smooth, coherent transitions where a straight line cuts through off-manifold states. Their evaluation is in sample or along a behaviour manifold. That leaves open whether curve steering works for a continuous physical variable, at values the curve never saw, when the judge is a video model's own forecast. The why slide carries the same two questions in the main talk.
