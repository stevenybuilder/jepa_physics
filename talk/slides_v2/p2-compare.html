This is the README's comparison of spline steering with the Part 1 multi-probe method, plus the conceptor gate of COAST (Miao et al. 2605.17144; Jaeger 1403.3369) as the multiplicative alternative to an additive shift. Multi-probe: a linear subspace where the variable is read and a fixed additive step; it reaches the target with a handful of probes and disturbs speed least, but it needs labelled probes and a single plain probe fails at 78 degrees. Chord: the straight line between two raw class means; it lands closest of the geometric arms but crosses the ring's hollow, readout radius 0.63 here and 0.23 at the midpoint for the largest shifts, and it moves speed three times the natural spread. Two splines, kept apart: the smoothing spline in the table, whose smoothed knots pull the endpoint off the held-out means (9.7 degrees) and whose block-22 edit leaves the forecast 44.2 degrees from the target, and the interpolating spline through the raw centroids, which gives the best forecast of any arm at block 22, 11.3 degrees (results/session2_predictor_native_readout.json per_layer.22.{spline_smooth,spline,chord,probe_qr}.dir_err_to_target: 44.2, 11.3, 27.2, 17.1). Both keep every waypoint on the ring; on straight variables, speed and acceleration, neither adds anything over the chord. Conceptor: source and target conceptors, each a soft projector onto the class's covariance ellipsoid, combined with COAST's NOT-source AND target and applied as a gate with the aperture chosen by COAST's own overlap rule (Appendix A.10.2; selected 0.1, no aperture in the 0.85 to 0.95 band). At block 12 it lands 165 degrees from the target and moves speed by 15 metres per second; at block 22 its best arm reaches 41.8 degrees (interval 38.9 to 44.5) against the chord's 3.6. The reason is geometric, not a tuning problem: a conceptor encodes the shape of a value's cloud, not where it sits, and neighbouring directions have clouds of nearly the same shape, so target AND NOT source keeps almost nothing, a trace of 0.66 out of 64 at block 12 (arms.coast_a.trace_C_mean); COAST's pseudoinverse AND is not even a valid conceptor for 96 percent of pairs (aperture.pinv_AND_invalid_frac_by_alpha), so Jaeger's AND was used, and only 1 to 14 percent of the conceptor edit lies in the ring plane against about 60 percent for the spline and chord. Rescaling cannot rotate a point around a ring; that needs a displacement, and the ring decides which displacement.
