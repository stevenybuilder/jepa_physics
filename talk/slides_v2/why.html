Why this question

Sources: refs/physics_paper.txt C.1.4, Table 1; refs/steering_paper.txt §1, §7. ~50 s. V-JEPA 2's predictor forecasts the latent future and V-JEPA 2-AC plans with such forecasts, so to steer the model we need to know what the predictor consumes, not only what a probe reads. Joseph et al. showed motion becomes readable by blocks 8 to 9, the Physics Emergence Zone. Wurgaft et al. showed curved codes steer best along their curve. Neither asked whether the predictor uses the code, and curve steering was never tested on a physical variable, at unseen values, judged by a video forecast.
