Controls throughout: same-rank random bases, rank-matched random directions, shuffled labels, an untrained copy of the encoder.

Probes

1,200 train and 300 test clips per variable; five folds

Endpoint bake-off

16 seeds, 15 held-out arcs × 8 targets × 48 clips; paired clip bootstrap resolves 0.1° to 0.2°

Through the predictor

200 carriers × 4 held-out targets; carrier bootstrap resolves about 2.5°; position 48 × 2

Held-out rule

knot clips build the curve, probe clips fit the reader, test clips are edited; held-out values never enter the knots
