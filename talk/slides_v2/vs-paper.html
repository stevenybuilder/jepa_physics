Against the two papers: what reproduces and what does not

Joseph et al. · probing

Emergence zone, tens of probes, 18 probes to steer: reproduce

"Speed needs fewer probes" holds under one stop rule only

New: the zone is where the code stops depending on position

Wurgaft et al. · manifold steering

Spline stays on the manifold, chord crosses the hollow: reproduce

At held-out targets the raw chord beats the spline, 4.7° to 9.7°

New: the forecast, not the probe, separates the two paths

Comparison

~60 s. Left, the physics paper (Joseph et al. 2602.07050). Reproduces: the pooled emergence zone at blocks 6 to 8 on their own data, per-patch R squared 0.96 at block 6 on the supplied render; every variable needs tens of probes to erase at the paper's layer, outside the random-removal band; the paper's own probe recipe steers direction to within 10 degrees with 18 probes, against their about 20. Does not reproduce cleanly: their claim that speed needs fewer probes than direction depends on the stop rule; on the same clips it holds in 7 of 8 cells under Appendix C.11's rule and in 0 of 8 under Figure 22's caption thresholds. What we add: measured per patch against an untrained copy of the network, the zone is not where direction first becomes decodable but where the code stops depending on where the disk is. Right, the manifold-steering paper (Wurgaft et al. 2605.05115). Reproduces: on a ring the spline keeps every waypoint on the manifold and the chord between class means crosses the hollow centre, readout radius 0.23 at the midpoint for large shifts. Does not: the paper's linear baseline, the raw chord between class means, lands closer to a held-out target than the smoothing spline at the endpoint, 4.7 against 9.7 degrees at block 12 and 3.6 against 10.7 at block 22, and over the 16 arc positions the chord leads by 1.3 and 2.3 degrees; on straight variables, speed and acceleration, the spline adds nothing. What we add: the judge. Read through V-JEPA 2's predictor at block 22, the spline's forecast follows the intermediate directions while the chord's jumps, and mid-depth edits from either method are erased within four blocks by attention and MLP alike. Sources are the ones on the results slides; the split-rule numbers are results/p1b_sameclip_direction_vs_speed.json.
