Zone: Joseph et al. 'refer to this consistent one-third depth transition as the Physics Emergence Zone', with 'a sharp transition between Layers 7 and 8' in ViT-L; our zone tests use blocks 8 and 9. Predictor: a separate transformer downstream of the encoder that reads the encoder's output tokens of frames 1–8 and forecasts the representations of frames 9–16; it is not interleaved with the encoder, and V-JEPA 2-AC is not used. Navy marks readouts, terracotta interventions. One: ridge probes per block and per patch. Two: an additive edit at one block (chord, multi-probe, spline, conceptor). Three: swap the disk's tokens between clips. Four: how later blocks undo the edit. Five: a probe on the forecast, fit on disjoint clips, the rendered future as ceiling. Six: the edit on the disk's tokens only.
