Methods

Sources: methods.html. ~40 s. V-JEPA 2 ViT-L/16 at 256 pixels, frozen, read at 26 depths, each a mean over 2,048 tokens. Ridge probes with stratified 5-fold cross-validation inside train, one 80/20 split per set; grouped folds appear only in p1a_grouped_cv.json. Supplied clips: 1,500 direction, 1,536 speed, 1,536 acceleration. Curves: PCA to 64 dimensions on training clips, one centroid per value, a periodic spline for direction and natural splines for the scalars. An edit moves a clip along the curve and keeps its off-curve residual.
