Reading and steering motion in V-JEPA 2

Where a frozen video encoder keeps direction, speed and acceleration, and which edits change what its predictor forecasts

Steven Yang · September 2026

Sources: talk/slides_v2/NUMBERS.md. ~15 s. Part 1 reproduces Joseph et al.'s probing on the frozen V-JEPA 2 encoder. Part 2 asks what the world model does with the code. Three findings: heading reaches the forecast through the object's tokens until late; at matched rank the code prefers polar to x–y, but only after the zone; and a curved edit keeps the heading along the path and, through the predictor at block 22, forecasts closer than a straight edit, while in the encoder the straight edit lands as close.
