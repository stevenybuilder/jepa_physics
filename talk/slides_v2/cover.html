V-JEPA 2 physics take-home

Steering a video world model
along the shape of its physics

Geometry-aware steering, judged by what V-JEPA 2 does with the edit

Steven Yang · September 2026

~15 s. Part 1 reproduces the probing methodology of Joseph et al. on the frozen V-JEPA 2 ViT-L/16 encoder. Part 2 is the open-ended extension, organised around one thesis: a steering edit should follow the geometry a variable actually has in the encoder, and it should be judged by what the world model does with it, not by a probe at the edited layer. Part 2 asks four questions in order: what shape each variable has, which steering method respects that shape at held-out targets, whether the predictor obeys the edit and why not at mid depth, and whether it generalises across renders. Every number on the slides is read from a results file named in the speaker notes and in talk/slides_v2/NUMBERS.md.
