Results · Part 2

~55 s. Source: results/p5_time_manifold.json (clock, geometry, decode, steer), direction set, block 12 unless stated. Within a clip each of the 8 time steps has its own pooled feature. Subtract the clip's mean over steps and the 8 residuals trace a path. The step index is decodable from that residual with test R squared 1.00 from block 8 on (decode, t_r2). The random-init encoder also decodes it at 0.97, so this may partly be the encoder's token-time position code, and decodability alone says little; the shape and the rate carry the result. Geometry: a line in t explains 0.15 of held-out clips' residual variance, and the step centroids and the spline through them explain 0.77 (geometry.heldout_var_explained), so the path is a curve, not a line. The speed test: fit a time spline through the step centroids of mid-speed knot clips, then read each held-out clip's steps as the nearest point on it. On slow, mid and fast held-out clips the coordinate advances 1.003, 0.998 and 0.996 per step (clock["direction/vjepa2/timepool"]["12"].spline.{slow,mid,fast}.slope.mean). A code that tracked distance travelled would advance 0.38 per step for the slow band and 1.63 for the fast band (odometer_prediction). The untrained copy gives 0.51, 0.58 and 0.94 (clock["direction/random/timepool"]), which matches neither. The same holds at blocks 8, 19 and 22 and on the speed and acceleration sets. So the code is speed-invariant and counts frames. What it does not show: at the fixed 24 frames per second, step index, frame count and elapsed seconds are one variable, so "elapsed time" in physical units and "keeps a clock" are not established. Steering: moving a step's residual along the time spline sets the decoded step to within 0.10, against 2.43 unedited. The raw chord does the same, 0.09 (steer.12.summary). The controls: the time-rescaled renders are speed changes in disguise, because at a fixed frame rate x(λt) is the clip with speed λv, and 168 of 392 clips per λ are identical to paper-layout clips. They test probe linearity, not a clock. The frozen speed readout doubles as a linear readout predicts inside the probe's training range, 0.98 to 1.03 observed over expected at points 9 to 22, and saturates above 4 metres per second, 0.95 to 0.97 over all in-frame clips (results/p5_clock_test_linearity.json; results/CLOCK_NOTES.md). A static-disk control was not run, because no zero-speed clips exist in the supplied sets (results/p5_time_manifold_controls.json static_disk_control). On the speed set's 768-clip subset the untrained copy decodes the step at R squared 0.97 against V-JEPA 2's 1.00, and its per-step slopes are 0.71, 0.74 and 0.77 at block 22 against 0.99, 1.00 and 1.00 (results/p5_time_manifold_controls.json).
