Limitations

~35 s. Scope first. Everything is one orange disk on a flat floor with a fixed camera; the harder render is one step away from that, 392 clips per seed, 8 directions, three render seeds. Per-patch probes use features averaged over the 8 time steps and one pooled probe per half-frame. Acceleration clips start at rest, so acceleration, mean speed and displacement are one variable. The predictor readout is a linear probe on the pooled forecast, not a rendered future; the four targets sit in one 45-degree arc, and the repair attribution uses 16 carriers and one target. The Part 2 verdict rule was iterated during development and frozen before the arc sweep; point 12 is an exploratory layer choice. The rank-74 random-basis null is conservative and the rank-matched one is reported beside it. The conceptor comparison uses COAST's aperture rule as published; no aperture fell in its band, so a tuned conceptor could do better at the endpoint, though not around the ring. Next: more renders and seeds, per-token patch probes and VideoMAE per patch, to see whether the negative half-frame transfer on the hard render is about rendering or about latent prediction; intermediate training checkpoints, since random init and the final model are the only two points on the training axis here; a stimulus the predictor tracks over longer horizons with targets spread around the ring; decoding the steered forecast to pixels; and multi-object, occluded scenes. Reproducibility: every number is read from a JSON in results/ that carries its split hash, git commit and seeds; V-JEPA 2 extraction took 38 minutes on a rented RTX 4080 SUPER for 0.19 dollars, and everything else runs on stored activations on a laptop CPU, which is why 32 held-out arcs, two splits and every control were affordable.
