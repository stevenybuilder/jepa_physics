The two papers

Sources: refs/physics_paper.txt C.1.4, Table 1; refs/steering_paper.txt §1, §7. ~50 s. Joseph et al. showed direction, speed and acceleration are readable layer by layer, that tens of probes are needed to erase direction, and that probe edits steer a probe's readout. They left open whether velocity is bound to the object, and never asked whether the predictor consumes the code. Wurgaft et al. showed that edits along a curve fitted to class means beat straight vectors, in sample, on language and RL tasks. Open: a continuous physical variable, values the curve never saw, judged by the model's own forecast. The rest of the talk answers these four.
