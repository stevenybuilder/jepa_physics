How to check anything on these slides

Every number is read from a JSON in results/ and each JSON carries its split hash, git commit and seeds. Committed split files, frame-hash grouping, TF32 off, 218 tests. Real-data runs come from frozen worktrees at a named commit, and the paper's 70/30 split was rerun for both parts as a check.

Compute

V-JEPA 2 extraction on a rented RTX 4080 SUPER (38 minutes, $0.19); VideoMAE, random-init, the predictor session and the per-patch probes on an RTX 4060 Ti ($1.51 recorded). Everything else runs on stored pooled activations on a laptop CPU. A Part 2 run takes minutes, and that is why I could afford 32 held-out arcs, two splits and every control.
