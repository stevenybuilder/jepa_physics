Reproducibility, cost, and where to argue with me

How to check anything on these slides

Every number is read from a JSON in results/ and each JSON carries its split hash, git commit and seeds. Committed split files, frame-hash grouping, TF32 off, 173 tests. Real-data runs come from frozen worktrees at a named commit. An independent re-derivation from the raw videos and the raw forward pass matched Part 1 to the printed decimal and Part 2 within 0.01.

Compute

One extraction pass per model on a rented RTX 4060 Ti, then everything on stored pooled activations on a laptop CPU. Total GPU spend about one dollar. Part 2 runs take minutes each, which is what made 32 held-out arcs, two splits and every control affordable.

Three claims I would defend, one I would not

Defend: on this stimulus availability is not the interesting finding; direction is a stretched rank-2 ring with a nonlinear residual; late edits along the ring reach the predictor's forecast. Not defend: that any of this transfers to Kubric-scale scenes without the per-patch and checkpoint experiments.
