What a meaningful held-out steering evaluation is: three leaks, three separations

The spline memorises its knots

Steering targets are values the spline never had as knots. Main design: a contiguous 45° arc of 8 directions held out, repeated over 15 distinct arcs. Scattered hold-out is run and labelled as unable to separate chord from arc. Extrapolation is run and labelled as extrapolation.

The readout is the intervention

Three disjoint clip sets: 632 knot clips build the spline, 480 probe clips fit the evaluation probe and the MLP, 300 test clips are steered. Two further readouts never built the edit: agreement with real clips at the target value, and the predictor.

The edit looks right only at layer L

Goodfire's energy at the steered layer restates the geometry steered along, and every figure says so. The answer lives downstream: propagation to later layers and the predictor's own forecast.

Why held-out values are the crux: at a value the spline saw, the chord endpoint and the curve endpoint coincide, so linear and spline steering cannot be told apart at the endpoint. At a held-out value they differ by the sagitta of the arc, and the path between them differs everywhere.

Both arms edit the same PCA-64 subspace and keep each clip's off-subspace residual (matched support). Controls: dose-matched line, projected and reflected arms, 20 endpoint-matched random curves, 20 shuffled-centroid curves, a BF16 repeat. The verdict rule was frozen before the arc sweep and applied unchanged to all 32 runs.
