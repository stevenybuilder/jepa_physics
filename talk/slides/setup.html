Setup

Model and readout

  • V-JEPA 2 ViT-L/16, 256², frozen, fp32
  • 26 points: embedding, blocks 1–24, final norm
  • Mean over 2,048 tokens, as in the paper
  • Ridge probes, α by 5-fold CV

Data and split

  • One orange disk, fixed camera
  • 1,500 direction clips (64 angles), 1,536 speed, 1,536 acceleration
  • One 80/20 split per set, stratified by value
  • 5 folds inside train for every choice

What held-out means

  • Clips: the evaluation probe never saw the fit clips
  • Values: targets the spline never had as knots
  • Context: speed-set clips steered with a direction spline

Deviations from the paper sit in one table in the report. Where the paper allows two readings, both are run; Part 1 and the Part 2 steering at the paper's 70/30 change no verdict.
