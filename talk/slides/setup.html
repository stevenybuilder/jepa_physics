Setup

Model and readout

V-JEPA 2 ViT-L/16 at 256², frozen, fp32, 26 hidden-state points (embedding, blocks 1–24, final norm). Mean over 2,048 tokens, as in the paper. Ridge probes with α by 5-fold CV; direction fit to (sin θ, cos θ) and scored circularly.

Data and split

One orange disk, fixed camera. 1,500 direction clips (64 angles), 1,536 speed, 1,536 acceleration. One 80/20 split per set, stratified by value, 5 folds inside train for every choice. Parts 1 and 2 rerun at the paper's 70/30: no verdict changes.

What held-out means here

Held-out clips: the evaluation probe never saw the fit clips. Held-out values: steering targets the spline never had as knots. Held-out context: speed-set clips steered with a direction spline. Each result is labelled with the one it meets.

The deviations from the paper sit in one table in the report (ridge vs Adam with a parity check, 256² input, 26 points, 64 directions, split). Where the paper contradicts itself (stopping threshold 0.1 vs 0.3, 80/20 vs 70/30, evaluation probe fit on the steered clips), I ran both readings.
