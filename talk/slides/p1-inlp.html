The paper's plotted Fig. 22 reads about 44 direction and 28 speed probes at layer 8 and its Fig. 23 speed curve ends near R² 0.05, so for speed the figure follows the method-text thresholds; for direction Fig. 23's layer-8 run reaches about 100 probes with accuracy still 20–60% near probe 44, so the 44 came from an earlier cutoff (the caption's 0.3 fits: our stored-set count under it is 42 at point 8), and read that way, caption for direction and C.11 for speed, speed needs more on the same clips (11 vs 26, 19 vs 28 at point 9).
