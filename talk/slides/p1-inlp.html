Removing the same number of random dimensions leaves R² at 0.980, so the count means something. Scoring each round on the test split, as the paper does, gives K = 46 / 45 / 47; the nested count fits the whole sequence inside each train fold and never reads test.

Under ridge neither variable shows a sawtooth. With the paper's literal Adam recipe both curves go jagged and K roughly doubles, for speed as much as for direction on 8-bin accuracy. In raw coordinates K is 1.4–1.6× larger and still equal at point 9 (51 vs 55).
