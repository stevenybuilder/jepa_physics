The result first: late edits along the direction ring reach the predictor's forecast; early ones do not

Causal

Steer direction at block 22 toward a held-out value and the predictor forecasts the disk moving that way: 11–27° from target against 92° unedited and a 180° null at 150–170°. At matched edit size the on-ring path lands 8–13° closer than the straight one. Steer at block 12 or earlier and the network repairs the edit within four blocks.

Two disagreements with the paper

No emergence zone on the supplied clips: all three variables decode from block 1, in a random-init network too. No direction-specific sawtooth. Both diagnosed: sample size, stimulus, and the probe-fitting recipe.

Two agreements, one step past

Tens of probes per variable, and the steering shape (one probe fails, five reach the target). Past the paper: direction is a stretched rank-2 ring the model finds without labels, and at held-out arcs the spline path stays on it while the chord cuts through.

Three claims I will defend: on this stimulus availability is not the finding; direction is a rank-2 ring plus a nonlinear residual; block-22 edits reach the forecast. One I will not: that any of it transfers to richer scenes without per-patch probes and training checkpoints.
