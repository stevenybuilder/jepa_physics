Per patch, the emergence zone depends on the stimulus

Supplied clips: early

Mean per-position R² 0.56 at block 1, 0.89 at block 5, 0.96 at block 6 (onset 5 [5, 5]); no step at points 8→9 (0.958 → 0.975). The pooled-patch probe works at every position from block 1 (0.83); half-frame transfer 0.82 at block 1, 0.96 at point 9, 0.81 at point 22.

Training-selective

The random-init network pools to 0.86–0.88 at every point, but its mean per-position R² never exceeds 0.39 (best single position 0.76–0.86) and half-frame transfer stays at 0.61–0.74: the paper's fragmented local signal that pooling adds up. V-JEPA 2 leaves that regime within six blocks.

Harder render: two readouts

Textured floor, shading, smaller disk, three render seeds (392 clips each): per-position R² rises most between points 4 and 6 on every seed (+0.24 to +0.29) and only +0.07 across 8→9; a random-init network sits at 0.5–0.6 from point 6 on the same clips. Half-frame transfer is 0.7 at point 1, negative at points 7–8 and back to about chance at 9 on every seed (a miscalibration between halves, not a mirror flip). The jump is at her depth (the dip starts at points 4–5) and the shape is absent in the random network; the rise is earlier. The paper-layout render: onset 6, transfer 0.81 at block 1.

Pooled availability is architectural here, as her rebuttal's 13 models suggest, and VideoMAE matches V-JEPA 2 on every pooled measure. What training adds is the per-patch code. Caveats: time-averaged per-position features, one pooled probe per half-frame, 10 sampled points on the rendered sets (13 on hard seeds 1–2), 7 shared start positions.
