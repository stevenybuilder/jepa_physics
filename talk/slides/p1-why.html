The paper's own C.5 says the jump is in per-patch probes and pooled probes rise gradually, so this is the test that matches her claim. The README warns that the supplied dataset is deliberately simpler, and that is where the difference is: gradual on the supplied clips; on the harder render the onset rule gives 8–9 on every seed while the largest step is early (4→6), and half-frame transfer dips and jumps back at her depth. I call that partial agreement: a per-patch zone at about her depth on a harder stimulus, not on the supplied one, plus a new cross-half dip. The paper-scale subsample I showed before (onset 5 at 150 clips) does not explain it: at 392 clips the pooled onset is back at 2. If she asks for her rebuttal's sigmoid criterion: the per-position curves inflect at points 5.7 to 5.9 on all three hard seeds, only the half-frame curves inflect at her point 9 and those fail its fit test, and the criterion accepts the random-init curves too.
