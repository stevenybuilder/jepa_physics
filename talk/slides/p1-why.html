Why there is no emergence zone here

Paper-scale n

Subsampling train to the paper's clip count (8 directions, ~150 clips) moves the direction onset from point 2 to point 5 [4, 5]. Part of the gap is sample size.

Make the stimulus harder

Re-rendered with a textured floor, shading and a smaller disk (392 clips, 8 directions): onset moves to point 5 [5, 7] and V-JEPA's block 1 drops below the random-init network. A partial recovery.

Not the objective

VideoMAE (pixel reconstruction) matches V-JEPA 2 (latent prediction) on all three variables: same onsets, peak 0.992 vs 0.991, 4 probes to steer direction under 10° for both. Latent prediction isn't what makes the variables available here.

Her rebuttal reports direction decodable in all 13 models she tested, so availability everywhere is her finding as well. What this data can speak to is what training adds: precision, fewer probes to steer, and a label-free ring (Part 2).
