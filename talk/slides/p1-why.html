Per patch, the emergence zone depends on the stimulus

Supplied clips: early

Mean per-position R² 0.56 at block 1, 0.89 at block 5, 0.96 at block 6 (onset 5 [5, 5]); no step at points 8→9 (0.958 → 0.975). The pooled-patch probe works at every position from block 1 (0.83); half-frame transfer 0.82 at block 1, 0.96 at point 9, 0.81 at point 22.

Training-selective

The random-init network pools to 0.86–0.88 at every point, but its per-position R² never exceeds 0.39 and half-frame transfer stays at 0.61–0.74: the paper's fragmented local signal that pooling adds up. V-JEPA 2 leaves that regime within six blocks.

Harder render: the step

Textured floor, shading, smaller disk (392 clips, one render seed): per-position onset 9 [9, 9], 0.851 → 0.944 across points 8→9; half-frame transfer −1.42 at 8, 0.08 at 9, 0.75 at 22 (a miscalibration between halves, not a mirror flip). The paper-layout render: onset 6, transfer 0.81 at block 1.

Pooled availability is architectural here, as her rebuttal's 13 models suggest, and VideoMAE matches V-JEPA 2 on every pooled measure. What training adds is the per-patch code. Caveats: time-averaged per-position features, one pooled probe per half-frame, 10 sampled points on the rendered sets, 7 shared start positions.
