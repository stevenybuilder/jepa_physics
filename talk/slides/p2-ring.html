Part 2. Direction is a ring; speed and acceleration are lines

Goodfire's recipe: PCA-64 on train, one centroid per value, a periodic spline for direction and a natural spline for scalars. I read the angle as atan2 of the top two components without using labels, and only then compare it with the truth.

Point 12: circular correlation −0.98 with the true angle. Point 22: −0.96. At point 8 the ring can't be recovered, and the file records that. Fitted in its own plane the ring is an ellipse, axis ratio 0.74 to 0.87, bent out of plane by a saddle; the top-2 PC plane sometimes shows that fold as a bent line.

Speed and acceleration curves bend by 0.04 to 0.19 of the centroid noise. The speed axis is the same at every direction: a cylinder, not a cone.

So in the ring's plane a straight step from 0° toward 180° goes through the centre, where no real clip lives: held-out clips fill the ring (largest gap 2.9°) and the chord midpoint sits at radius 0.09.
