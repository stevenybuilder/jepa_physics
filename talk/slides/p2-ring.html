Cut from the slide: Goodfire's recipe: PCA-64 on train, one centroid per value, a periodic spline for direction and a natural spline for scalars. Goodfire's text reads the angle as atan2 of the top two components without labels; its weekdays and months 8B runs do the same; only its 70B cyclic runs use the labels. I try the label-free angle first and check it against the truth. Point 12: circular correlation −0.98 with the true angle, and it holds on every arc's knot clips. Point 22: −0.96 on all train clips, but on the knot clips it fails on the headline arc and 12 of 16 arcs (0.50 centroid plane, 0.81 activation plane), so point 22 steers on labels. Label-free recovery is my extension and works at point 12 only. Fitted in its own plane the ring is an ellipse, axis ratio 0.68 to 0.89 from point 6 on, bent out of plane by a saddle; the top-2 PC plane sometimes shows that fold as a bent line. Acceleration clips all start at rest, so the two lines are one experiment. The speed axis is the same at every direction: a cylinder, not a cone. Held-out clips fill the ring (largest gap 2.9°).
