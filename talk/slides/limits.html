Limitations and what I would run next

Limitations

  • One disk, one background, a fixed camera. The hard set has 392 clips and 8 directions.
  • Mean-pooled probes throughout; her sharp per-patch onset was not tested.
  • Acceleration clips start at rest, so acceleration, mean speed and displacement are one variable.
  • The predictor readout is a linear probe on the pooled forecast, not a rendered future; four targets sit in one 45° arc; position overshoots.
  • The Part 2 verdict rule was iterated during development, then frozen before the arc sweep. Point 12 is an exploratory layer choice.
  • The random-basis null with rank 74 is conservative; the rank-matched one is reported beside it.

Next

  • Per-patch probes at every layer, to test whether the emergence step is a pooling artefact on this stimulus.
  • Intermediate training checkpoints: the two points here (random init, final) are the whole training-dynamics axis available.
  • A stimulus the predictor tracks over longer horizons, with targets spread around the ring, and a rendered-future readout.
  • Steering at point 22 for speed and acceleration through the predictor, where the manifolds are straight and the two methods should coincide.
  • Multi-object and occluded scenes, where object permanence can be separated from attention mixing.
