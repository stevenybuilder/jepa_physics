Limitations and what I would run next

Limitations

  • Her paper's own inconsistencies, read literally and reported both ways: stop thresholds 0.1 / 0.05 in C.11 vs 0.3 / 0.1 in the Fig. 22 caption; 80/20 in C.11 vs 70/30 in C.12; C.12 fits its evaluation probe on the clips it then steers; under 0.5° with all probes in the text, citing an appendix that reports only 11.9° held-out with 20.
  • One disk, one background, a fixed camera. The half-frame dip shows only on the hard set: 392 clips per seed, 8 directions, three render seeds, 7 shared start positions per seed.
  • Per-patch probes use features averaged over the 8 time steps and one pooled probe per half-frame; the rendered sets were read at 10 points, so their early onsets are upper bounds.
  • Acceleration clips start at rest, so acceleration, mean speed and displacement are one variable.
  • The predictor readout is a linear probe on the pooled forecast, not a rendered future; four targets sit in one 45° arc; position overshoots. The spline's matched-norm lead is a block-22 result; at the encoder output the lead shrinks to 2° at the natural norm and reverses at the chord's norm.
  • The Part 2 verdict rule was iterated during development, then frozen before the arc sweep. Point 12 is an exploratory layer choice.
  • The random-basis null with rank 74 is conservative; the rank-matched one is reported beside it.

Next

  • More hard renders and seeds, per-token patch probes, and VideoMAE per patch, to see whether the half-frame dip and jump at points 4–9 is about the rendering or about latent prediction.
  • Intermediate training checkpoints: the two points here (random init, final) are the whole training-dynamics axis available.
  • A stimulus the predictor tracks over longer horizons, with targets spread around the ring, and a rendered-future readout.
  • Steering at point 22 for speed and acceleration through the predictor, where the manifolds are straight and the two methods should coincide.
  • Multi-object and occluded scenes, where object permanence can be separated from attention mixing.
