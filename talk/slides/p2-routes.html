Two things only a manifold can do, and one thing fewer probes cannot

0° to 180° by two routes

Via 90° the MLP readout climbs +89°; via 270° it falls −94°. The chord expresses only one route and its midpoint radius collapses to 0.14. A line cannot say which way round.

Fewer probes, at matched edit size

Her open question. A rank-2 rotation in the ring plane reaches 16.9° (linear readout) and 36.7° (MLP) against 5.6° and 23.1° for the probe-QR subspace. The ring is where direction is read, not the whole of where it is written: the moves need the higher-rank edit.

Donor ceiling: a real clip's in-subspace activation transplanted into the carrier reaches R ≈ 0.20–0.23; the spline reaches 82–84% of that ceiling. About 40% of the spline's edit is shared with the probe subspace's edit.
