## B. Proposed additions to nonobvious_components.md

1. **Item 1, acceleration bullet:** replace "reproducible only in this weakened sense" with "the paper has the same confound (App. A.1.2), so its 'no velocity intermediate' claim is untested by its own data and by ours" (as spec #3).
2. **Item 1, new bullet, "Is direction Cartesian or polar?"** (spec #6). A 64 × 64 design can test Table 1's claim directly.
3. **Item 3, new bullet, "the evaluation probe's own R² must be out-of-fold"** (spec #5), and **"steer to a radius-matched target"** (spec #4).
4. **Item 4, new bullet, "cone or cylinder, not ring":** the speed set crosses 64 speeds with 64 directions, 24 clips per speed. If the ring's radius changes with speed, the right object is Goodfire's cylinder task: a thin-plate spline over (θ, speed) with ghost points one period above and below θ (`refs/steering_paper.txt` L2640–2647, and the 'concentric circles' result at L3255–3257). This is a stronger Part 2 than three separate 1-D splines.
5. **Item 4, new bullet, "speed may also be curved":** the paper's speed GLM is quadratic because neurons have *preferred speeds* (C.8, L1128–1150). A population of peaked tuning curves traces a curved centroid path. Do not predict "speed is straight" before the leave-one-out test.
6. **Item 5, point 1:** state that every-4th holdout is a local interpolation test (11.25° gaps), and add the contiguous-arc and extrapolation designs (spec #1). Also add a **held-out context** design: steer speed-set clips (unseen speeds and start range) with a direction spline built on the direction set.
7. **Item 5, new point 4, "three meanings of held-out"** (spec #2), citing `js/methods_comparison.md`.
8. **Item 6, random-curve control:** use ≥ 20 draws, and separate a random curve with its own fit from the learned coefficients on a random frame (spec #10). Also report **per-clip rescue and harm counts** next to the net effect. jepa_steering's Reach rank-4 edit rescued 23 native failures and broke 17 successes (`js/wm-approaches.md` §6). On Reach-Wall, a history estimator won in 93 of 128 families yet lost on average (`js/findings.md`, overnight follow-up).
9. **Item 6, new bullet, the subspace-patching illusion:** an edit can move the readout through a pathway the model does not use for the variable (Makelov, Lange & Nanda, arXiv 2311.17030, cited in `js/matched random control.md` §3). "MAE-to-true rises" does not rule this out. The later-layer agreement readout (item 5.3) is the check.
10a. **Item 6, new bullet, matched support** (spec #1b): Goodfire's linear arm replaces the whole activation, while its manifold arm keeps the off-PCA residual. Our comparison edits the same subspace and keeps the same residual in both arms.
10b. **Item 7, new bullet, planted-ring positive control:** before reading any "no ring" or "no curvature" result, plant a synthetic ring of known radius in real activations and check that the pipeline recovers it (intrinsic angle, leave-one-out cubic gain). jepa_steering's archived geometry loop ran exactly this: "Planted rotating-code test passes, so the method was sensitive" (`js/docs/archive/legacy-branches-2026-09-04/root-plans/LOOP1_SUMMARY.md` L24). That is what let it report its null as a null.
10c. **Item 4, new bullet, "the global mean of a ring is its centre":** a mean-difference or single-vector steer toward "direction" averages to ≈ 0. jepa_steering built its rotating-code detector for this reason, and it also fitted a circular chart X ≈ μ + A[cos θ, sin θ] with the phase decoded by atan2 (`JEPA_STEERING_LESSONS.md` §6, P1). Use that chart as the supervised check on Goodfire's unsupervised atan2(PC2, PC1).
10. **Item 7, new bullet:** direction-vs-speed principal angles against k_A/d (spec #9). This comes before any off-target claim.
11. **Item 10, framing:** jepa_steering's local synthesis sets a bar worth quoting: a semantic axis needs "to predict a specified physical variable on held-out examples, and interventions must change the targeted variable while preserving appropriate others"; a manifold claim additionally needs "a fitted chart/metric and an on-manifold versus matched linear comparison" (`js/paper/research_synthesis.md` §3).
